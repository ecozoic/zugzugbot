# Holy Prism (Paladin)

Fires a beam of light that scatters to strike a clump of targets. 

If the beam is aimed at an enemy target, it deals 2,498 Holy damage and radiates 2,580 healing to 5 allies within 30 yds.

If the beam is aimed at a friendly target, it heals for 5,890 and radiates 934 Holy damage to 5 enemies within 30 yds.

Generates 3 Holy Power.
