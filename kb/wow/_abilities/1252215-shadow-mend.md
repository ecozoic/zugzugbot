# Shadow Mend (Priest)

Shadow Word: Pain damage has a chance to upgrade your next Flash Heal to Shadow Mend. Stacks up to 2 charges.

 Shadow Mend:
Wraps an ally in shadows which heal for 6,824, but at a price.

The ally will take 342 damage every 1 sec, until they have taken 3,361 total damage from all sources, or leave combat.
