# Celestial Conduit (Monk)

The August Celestials empower you, causing you to radiate 378 Nature damage onto enemies and 20,621 healing onto up to 5 injured allies within 15 yds over 3.6 sec, reduced beyond 5 targets.

You may move while channeling, but casting other healing or damaging spells cancels this effect.
