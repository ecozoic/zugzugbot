# Collapsing Star (Demon hunter)

While in Void Metamorphosis, every 30 Soul Fragments harvested grants access to Collapsing Star.

 Collapsing Star
Channel an immense amount of void energy into a single, cataclysmic blast, dealing 2,102 Cosmic damage to your target and 1,603 Cosmic damage to all nearby enemies. Damage reduced beyond 8 targets.

While casting Collapsing Star, your Fury drain is significantly reduced.
