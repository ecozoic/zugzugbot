# Ancestral Awakening (Shaman)

When you heal with your Healing Wave or Riptide you have a 30% chance to summon an Ancestral spirit to aid you, instantly healing an injured friendly party or raid target within 40 yards for 25% of the amount healed. Critical strikes increase this chance to 60%.
