# Atonement (Priest)

Power Word: Shield, Flash Heal, Plea, Penance, and Power Word: Radiance apply Atonement to your target for 15 sec.

Your spell damage heals all targets affected by Atonement for 28% of the damage done. Healing is reduced when healing more than 5 targets.
