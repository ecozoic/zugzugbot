# Dream Flight (Evoker)

Take in a deep breath and fly to the targeted location, healing all allies in your path for 1,900 immediately, and 4,283 over 15 sec, reduced beyond 5 targets.

Removes all root effects. You are immune to movement impairing and loss of control effects.

Damage taken while you are flying is absorbed, up to 100% of your maximum health.
