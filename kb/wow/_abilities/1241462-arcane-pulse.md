# Arcane Pulse (Mage)

A pulse of Arcane magic erupts from underneath your target, dealing 350 Arcane damage to all enemies within 2 yds. Damage reduced beyond 5 targets.

Each Arcane Charge increases damage by 84%, radius by 2 yds, and mana cost by 100%.

Generates 1 Arcane Charge.
