# Defy Fate (Evoker)

Fatal attacks are diverted into a nearby timeline, preventing the damage, and your death, in this one.

The release of temporal energy restores 9,715 health to you, and 3,334 to 4 nearby allies, over 9 sec. Healing starts high and declines over the duration.

May only occur once every 6 min.
