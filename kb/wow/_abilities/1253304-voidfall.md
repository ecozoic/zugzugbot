# Voidfall (Demon hunter)

Consume has a 35% chance to grant 1 stack of Voidfall.

Upon reaching 3 stacks of Voidfall, Reap consumes a stack to reach into the Void and call down a meteor strike at your target's location, dealing 767 Cosmic damage to all enemies within 8 yards. Damage reduced beyond 8 targets.
