# Champion's Spear (Warrior)

Throw a spear at the target location, chaining all enemies in the area to the spear's location and dealing 84 Physical damage instantly and an additional 128 Physical damage over 6 sec. 

While the spear is active, activating this ability will cause you to leap to the spear, dealing 16 Physical damage to all targets.

All damage dealt reduced beyond 5 targets.

Generates 10 Rage.
