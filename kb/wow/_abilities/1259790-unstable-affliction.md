# Unstable Affliction (Warlock)

Afflicts the target with 1,072 Shadow damage over 8 sec. Multiple uses of this ability may overlap.

If dispelled, deals 12,375 damage to the dispeller and silences them for 4 sec. This damage is increased by 15% per stack on the target.

Generates 1 Soul Shard if the target dies while afflicted.
