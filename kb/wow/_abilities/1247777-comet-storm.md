# Comet Storm (Mage)

Casting Ray of Frost replaces your Ray of Frost with Comet Storm, calling down a series of 7 icy comets on and around the target, that deals up to 2,281 Frost damage to all enemies within 8 yds of its impacts.

Each Comet Shatters 1 stack of Freezing.
