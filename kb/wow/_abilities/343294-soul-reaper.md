# Soul Reaper (Death knight)

Rupture the soul of your enemy, dealing 156 Shadowfrost damage and causing the foe to take 20% increased damage from your diseases and minions for 8 sec.

Only usable on enemies below 35% maximum health.

Consumes up to 2 available charges to cast Putrefy.
