# Howling Blast (Death knight)

Blast the target with a frigid wind, dealing 7 Frost damage to that foe, and reduced damage to all other enemies within 10 yards, infecting all targets with Frost Fever.

 Frost Fever
A disease that deals 59 Frost damage over 24 sec and has a chance to grant the Death Knight 4 Runic Power each time it deals damage.
