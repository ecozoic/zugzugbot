# Idol of Yogg-Saron (Priest)

After conjuring Shadowy Apparitions, gain a stack of Idol of Yogg-Saron. At 20 stacks, you summon a Thing from Beyond that casts Void Spike at nearby enemies for 20 sec.

 Void Spike
Hurls a bolt of dark magic, dealing 355 Shadow damage and 188 Shadow damage to all enemies within 10 yards of the target. Damage reduced beyond 5 targets.
