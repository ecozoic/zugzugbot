# Earth Shield (Shaman)

Protects the target with an earthen shield, increasing your healing on them by 20% and healing them for 328 when they take damage. This heal can only occur once every 3 sec. Maximum 9 charges.

Earth Shield can only be placed on one target at a time. Only one Elemental Shield can be active on the Shaman.
