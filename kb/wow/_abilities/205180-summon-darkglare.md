# Summon Darkglare (Warlock)

Summons a Darkglare from the Twisting Nether that increases the damage of your Agony, Corruption, and Unstable Affliction by 20% while active.

The Darkglare will serve you for 20 sec, blasting its target for 1,886 Shadow damage, increased by 10% for every damage over time effect you have active on their current target.
