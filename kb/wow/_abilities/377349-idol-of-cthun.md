# Idol of C'Thun (Priest)

Mind Flay has a chance to spawn a Void Tendril that channels Mind Flay or Void Lasher that channels Mind Sear at your target.

 Mind Flay
Assaults the target's mind with Shadow energy, causing 1,226 Shadow damage over 10 sec and slowing their movement speed by 30%.

Generates 5 Insanity over the duration.

 Mind Sear
Corrosive shadow energy radiates from the target, dealing 812 Shadow damage over 10 sec to all enemies within 10 yards of the target. Damage reduced beyond 5 targets.

Generates 5 Insanity over the duration.
