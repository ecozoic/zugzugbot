# Frozen Orb (Mage)

Launches an orb of swirling ice in front of you, dealing up to 5,137 Frost damage to all enemies it passes through over 15 sec. After a short delay, Frozen Orb will pursue its target. Deals reduced damage beyond 8 targets.

Enemies damaged by the Frozen Orb are slowed by 50% for 8 sec.
