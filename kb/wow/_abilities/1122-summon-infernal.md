# Summon Infernal (Warlock)

Summons an Infernal from the Twisting Nether, impacting for 226 Fire damage and stunning all enemies in the area for 2 sec.

The Infernal will serve you for 30 sec, dealing 213 damage to all nearby enemies every 1.8 sec and generating 1 Soul Shard Fragment every 1 sec.
