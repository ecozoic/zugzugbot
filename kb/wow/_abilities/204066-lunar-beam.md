# Lunar Beam (Druid)

Summons a beam of lunar light at your target's location, dealing 14 Arcane damage each second to your target and 14 to up to 5 other enemies within the beam. 

Lunar Beam also increases your mastery by 18%, and heals you for 788 over 8 sec.
