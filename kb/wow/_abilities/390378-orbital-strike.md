# Orbital Strike (Druid)

Celestial Alignment blasts all enemies in a targeted area for 1,016 Astral damage and applies Stellar Flare to them.

Reduces the cooldown of Celestial Alignment by 60 sec.

 Stellar Flare
Burns the target for 68 Astral damage, and then an additional 894 damage over 24 sec. If dispelled, causes 803 damage to the dispeller and blasts them upwards.

Generates 12 Astral Power.
