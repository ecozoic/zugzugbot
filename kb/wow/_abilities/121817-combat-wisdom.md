# Combat Wisdom (Monk)

While out of combat, your Chi balances to 2 instead of depleting to empty.

Every 15 sec, your next Tiger Palm also casts Expel Harm.

 Expel Harm
Expel negative chi from your body, healing for 463 and dealing 10% of the amount healed as Nature damage to an enemy within 20 yards.
