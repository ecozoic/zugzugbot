# Seed of Corruption (Warlock)

Embeds a demon seed in the enemy target that will explode after 10.7 sec, dealing 816 Shadow damage to all enemies within 10 yards and applying Corruption to them. Damage reduced beyond 8 targets.

The seed will detonate early if the target is hit by other detonations, or takes 190 damage from your spells.
