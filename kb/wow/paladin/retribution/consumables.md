## Gems
| Socket | Gem |
| --- | --- |
| Eversong Diamond | [Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983) |
| Other sockets | [Flawless Deadly Amethyst](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240898) |
| With [Loa Worshiper's Band](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251513) | [Flawless Masterful Garnet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240908) |

For maximum damage, you will want to use [Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983) as your Eversong Diamond, and [Flawless Deadly Amethyst](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240898) in all other sockets. Depending on your stats, other secondary stat gems can be reasonable alternatives, so sim your options just in case. If you have [Loa Worshiper's Band](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251513), you will likely instead want to use [Flawless Masterful Garnet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240908).
