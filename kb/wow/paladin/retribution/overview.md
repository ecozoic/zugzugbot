# Retribution Paladin — Overview

## Identity
Retribution Paladins are holy crusaders who use a Two-Handed weapon to turn the power of the Light into powerful damaging abilities and bring justice and vengeance to their enemies. Retribution is the damage specialization of Paladin and is a melee spec, although it has many abilities that can be cast from range as well.

The core of our rotation is centered around using abilities like **Blade of Justice** and **Judgment** to generate Holy Power, which is consumed to deal potent damage with abilities like **Final Verdict** or **Divine Storm**. Although Paladin's mobility options are somewhat limited, you will have access to a large array of buffs for yourself and your party to help you throughout a fight, as well as strong defensive and healing capabilities for your role.

If playing a melee DPS with strong group support, healing options, and powerful burst damage cooldowns sounds appealing, then Retribution might be for you.

## Strengths
* Easy to learn playstyle that doesn't punish mistakes harshly.
* Many strong defensive tools, which include both passive abilities and active ones like **Divine Shield** and **Divine Protection**.
* Plenty of utility options that will usually find uses in any type of content.
* Strong options for both self-healing and off-healing for your group if needed.

## Weaknesses
* On the low side for mobility; **Divine Steed** is your main option.
* Utility options can be redundant in high-end content.
* Low skill ceiling; there's not much nuance to master once you learn the basics.

## Hero Trees
Retribution has access to two Hero Talent trees: Templar and Herald of the Sun. Currently, Templar is slightly ahead on damage compared to Herald of the Sun, but the gap between them is not significant. You can comfortably play Herald of the Sun in any content as well.

### Herald of the Sun
Herald of the Sun Retribution Paladins are focused on periodic damage effects, especially their signature, **Dawnlight**. These effects also trigger automatically during your cooldowns. On top of that, Herald of the Sun also features buffs to **Divine Storm** and **Hammer of Wrath**.

### Templar
Templar Retribution Paladins gain access to **Hammer of Light**, a powerful Holy Power spender that you can use during your burst windows. You'll also rain down **Empyrean Hammers** from the sky upon your enemies, which are automatically cast through different spells and buffs.
