# Retribution Paladin — Stats

## Priority

Regardless of whether you are playing a Templar or Herald of the Sun build, your stat preferences will change very little. These priorities also remain roughly the same in AoE situations.

**Templar:**

1. Strength
2. Mastery
3. Critical Strike
4. Haste
5. Versatility

**Herald of the Sun:**

1. Strength
2. Mastery
3. Critical Strike
4. Haste
5. Versatility

Strength is your Primary stat, found on plate gear. It increases your Attack/Spell Power and damage/healing done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Retribution Paladin gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike:** Increases the chance to deal extra damage and healing done by virtually all abilities, including your own spells, auto attacks, trinkets, and many other effects. Gains value with talents like **Heart of the Crusader** and **Wrathful Descent** on AoE for Templar.
- **Haste:** Compresses the rotation by reducing both the GCD and the cooldown of the rotational abilities like **Crusader Strike**, **Judgment**, **Blade of Justice**, and **Hammer of Wrath**. Important for damage, but can be overrated due to it feeling more impactful than it sometimes is.
- **Mastery:** **Mastery: Highlord's Judgment** increases Holy damage done, which essentially buffs all of Retribution's natural damage. It also gives **Judgment** a chance to deal extra Holy damage when cast. Mastery affects all baseline spells and talents, but won't apply to racial abilities like **Light's Judgment** or other outside sources of Holy damage.
- **Versatility:** Increases damage done and reduces damage taken by half the amount. Although it's not a very exciting stat compared to the others, it will often be more valuable than it initially appears thanks to its damage reduction component.
