## Crafting
1. Craft [Spellbreaker's Bracers](https://www.wowhead.com/item=237834) and [Adherent's Silken Shroud](https://www.wowhead.com/item=239656) with [Arcanoweave Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240167)

Wrists and Cloak are the best slots to use embellishments on later on, since crafted items have a lower item level cap than regular items. Those slots contribute less Strength than other slots, so having a lower item level cap on them is less of a downside.

2. Optional: Craft [Loa Worshiper's Band](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251513)

This is a strong alternative embellishment for Retribution. When using it, you should use either all [Flawless Masterful Peridot](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240892) or all [Flawless Masterful Garnet](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240908) other than your [Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983) to maximize the potency of its effect, and also use [Stabilizing Gemstone Bandolier](https://www.wowhead.com/item=251490) as your second embellishment on another armor slot.

3. Replace Low Item Level Pieces.
