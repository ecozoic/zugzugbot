# Retribution Paladin — 11.1 Single-Target Rotation

<!-- TODO: replace placeholder with real Wowhead-distilled content -->

## Opener

Pre-pot Tempered Potion of Power 2 seconds before pull. Open with Wake of
Ashes followed by Crusader Strike, then Final Verdict to dump initial Holy
Power. Continue with the steady-state priority below once your opener
cooldowns are on the way back. Trinket usage depends on the encounter — for
a clean Patchwerk-style fight, slot both on-use trinkets into the opener
window so they line up with Avenging Wrath.

### Phase 1 (0-15s)

Hold Avenging Wrath for the second pull on Mythic+ if a Heroism window is
incoming on the next cooldown. Otherwise press it immediately after Wake of
Ashes for maximum overlap with your opener burst. Templar's Verdict should
be replaced with Final Verdict once you're at 4-5 Holy Power.

## Steady-State Priority

Cast Wake of Ashes on cooldown. Spend Holy Power on Final Verdict at 4-5
stacks. Use Crusader Strike and Blade of Justice to fill the gaps and
maintain the buff windows. Judgment generates 1 Holy Power and applies the
critical-strike debuff — keep it on cooldown. Hammer of Wrath comes online
at sub-20% on the boss; weave it in without dropping higher-priority spells.

## Cooldowns

Avenging Wrath every 60 seconds, lined up with Wake of Ashes when possible.
Crusade if specced as the alternative — preferred for sustained AoE on
trash pulls but slightly weaker on pure single-target. Don't forget Divine
Toll for 5 free Holy Power on demand during a damage burn window.
