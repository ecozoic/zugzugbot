# Protection Paladin — 11.1 Active Mitigation Priority

## Opener

Pre-pull with Avenging Wrath if the tank-swap timing allows it. Lead with
Avenger's Shield to apply the slow and pull ranged adds, then Judgment for
the damage debuff and Holy Power generation. Drop Consecration immediately
after positioning the pack — your damage profile depends on standing inside
it for the duration of the pull.

## Active Mitigation

Shield of the Righteous is your primary mitigation cooldown — keep at least
one charge banked at all times. Spend on incoming melee swings, especially
when you can see a tank-buster on the boss timeline. Word of Glory is the
panic button for big spike damage; cast it before you drop below 40% HP, not
after, since the heal lands on the next server tick.

### Cooldown Rotation

Ardent Defender for the largest unavoidable hits — boss casts, dungeon
mechanics with 200%+ swing damage. Guardian of Ancient Kings stacks
multiplicatively with Ardent Defender if you're really in trouble. Divine
Shield is the absolute last resort and drops your aggro for the duration.

## Group Utility

Blessing of Sacrifice on the healer during heavy pulls. Lay on Hands for
the DPS who pulled threat. Hand of Reckoning to peel adds back to you. Don't
forget Devotion Aura — it's passive, but it stacks with itself if multiple
Paladins are in the group, which matters in 2-Pally compositions.
