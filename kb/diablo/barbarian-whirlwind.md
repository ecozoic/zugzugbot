# Barbarian — Whirlwind Build (Season 32)

<!-- TODO: replace placeholder with real Maxroll-distilled content -->

## Skill Loadout

Whirlwind as your primary spender, paired with Lunging Strike as the
fury-generator and movement skill. Slot Rallying Cry for damage reduction
and resource regen, War Cry for the bonus damage multiplier, and Wrath of
the Berserker as your big cooldown. Sixth slot is flexible — Iron Skin for
a panic button or Ground Stomp for crowd control on elite packs.

## Paragon Priority

Heavy Handed glyph in the first board for the two-handed weapon damage
multiplier. Exploit on the second board to amplify damage against vulnerable
enemies. Wrath on the third for the resource generation bonus. Push for at
least Paragon 200 before swapping to the legendary nodes — Lust for
Carnage, then Sundering, then Hemorrhage in that order based on your
gear quality.

### Glyph Leveling

Prioritize leveling Heavy Handed and Exploit to glyph rank 21 first to hit
the additional multiplier breakpoints. Wrath can stay at 15 until you have
enough Paragon to pick up the surrounding magic nodes for the buff.

## Key Uniques

Ramaladni's Magnum Opus is mandatory for the Whirlwind build — it converts
the skill into a damage-on-demand burst rather than a sustained spin. Pair
with Ring of Red Furor for cooldown reduction on Wrath of the Berserker.
Tuskhelm of Joritz the Mighty rounds out the unique slots; the Berserk
extension is non-negotiable for endgame Pit pushing.
