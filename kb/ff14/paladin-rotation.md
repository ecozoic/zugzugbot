# Paladin — 7.1 Single-Target 60s Cycle

<!-- TODO: replace placeholder with real Balance-distilled content -->

## Opener

Pre-pull Holy Spirit at 1.5s before the boss is targetable to land the cast
exactly on pull. Follow with Fight or Flight, Imperator, then your weaponskill
combo: Fast Blade → Riot Blade → Royal Authority. Slot Circle of Scorn and
Expiacion in the second weave window for maximum oGCD coverage during the
buff phase. Confiteor follows immediately after Requiescat comes off cooldown.

### Confiteor Combo

Confiteor → Blade of Faith → Blade of Truth → Blade of Valor in strict
sequence. Each blade hits harder than the last; dropping the chain because of
movement or downtime is the single largest DPS loss in the rotation.

## 60-Second Cycle

Fight or Flight every 60 seconds drives the entire damage profile. Inside
the buff window, fit two MP-spending Holy Spirits, the full Confiteor combo,
and as many GCD weaves as your skill speed allows. Outside the window, the
priority is straightforward: combo into Royal Authority, drop Atonement
stacks generated by it, and dump Sword Oath uses on cooldown.

## oGCD Priority

Intervene gives you positional flexibility and a small damage multiplier —
use it on entry to a melee uptime window rather than as a movement tool.
Cover and Passage of Arms are tank utility cooldowns; coordinate with the
co-tank during raid mechanics. Sheltron has been replaced by Holy Sheltron
in Endwalker and beyond — generate Oath gauge through combo finishers and
spend it before each tank-buster lands.
